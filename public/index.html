02 — About

Jacob Gonzales

I'm a videographer, photographer, editor and motion designer. I don't just cut reels — I shoot events, interviews and portrait sessions, produce podcasts and talk shows end to end, build motion graphics and logo animations, and cut corporate and nonprofit film that has to represent a whole organisation, not just a moment.

Ongoing work spans podcast production for NRG Podcast, Coffee with Kelly and We Just Met; corporate film for CEO Discovery; nonprofit and event coverage for Sojourners, Children's Harbor, Clinics Can Help and the Literacy Coalition; plus gala interviews, custom motion graphics and branded social content.
